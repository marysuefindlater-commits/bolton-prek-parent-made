It opens full screen like an app, with the peach icon, and your checkmarks and notes stay saved on your phone.

  1. Open this page in Safari. If you got here from Instagram or a text, tap ••• and choose Open in Safari or Open in browser first.
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen. On newer iPhones it's inside the ••• menu.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave the name as ATL Eats and tap Add. Look for the peach on your home screen.
  1. Open this page in Chrome. If you're inside Instagram, tap ⋮ and choose Open in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app).
  4. Tap Add or Install. The peach icon will show up with your other apps.

Heads up: on iPhone, the home screen app keeps its own copy of your checkmarks, separate from Safari. If you already checked places off in Safari, tap Download backup at the bottom there, then Load a backup in the app.
